# VOL53: a counted cost bound for a 53-step SHA-256 ordinary collision

Score (collision-frontier-v5): time_log2 = 256.45311 target-compressions. Memory is reported separately: 2^19.82 bytes by construction (complete under the model; term max(0, log2-20) = 0).

## 1. Target and prices
- Target: two distinct messages under the standard IV, the first 53 steps, standard padding and feed-forward, all 256 output bits equal. Each message is one 64-byte data block followed by the common SHA-256 padding block; the two data blocks give an equal chaining value, so the equal padding block preserves the collision and the full messages collide.
- Prices: one 53-step target compression is one unit. Every other primitive word operation (add, sub, xor, and, or, not, shift, rotate, compare, random word, table index) costs 1/C, with C = 3988. C = 3988 is the organizer reference operation count of the 53-step compression (hash-smash/scripts/reference_operation_costs.py counting method run at 53 rounds; it gives 2896 at 40 rounds).

## 2. Head (experiment v53-shift-01)
- Generator: the VOL40 v7e generator with the W13 retry count raised from 14 to 22 outputs message pairs whose expanded difference follows the msb17 body (difference silent on words 27..39).
- Shift: each pair is replaced by its schedule words -13..2, computed backward from the expansion rule (W_-1 = W15 - sigma1(W13) - W8 - sigma0(W0), then W_-2 = W14 - sigma1(W12) - W7 - sigma0(W_-1), and so on to W_-13 = W3 - sigma1(W1) - W_-4 - sigma0(W_-12)). The resulting words 0..15 are each member's complete 64-byte data block (equal length; standard padding appends the same final block to both); both are run from the standard IV through all 53 steps using the unmodified SHA-256 schedule recurrence and constants K_i at their original indices i (no step, word, or constant is renumbered), so the claimed event is an ordinary collision of two complete messages. The expansion of the shifted pair is the original expansion moved by 13 words, so the difference is silent on words 40..52 (checked on every pair).
- The generator, the shift, the expansion and the recomputation of the shifted pair from the standard IV work bit-sliced on 256 lanes; a gate on 256 lanes counts as one word operation, the accounting of the board's R40 entry df1f1cd1. Early abort: 4 steps before the junction the batch checks a 32-bit necessary condition that needs only half of the next step (the output a - e of a step equals T2 - d, so the two members must differ there by exactly 2^31); a batch with a live lane then computes that step and checks the register-a and register-e differences (64 bits per lane, counted).
- Each kept pair costs 11,045.0 charged operations (2^1.46966 units): 2,758.491 word operations per kept pair = (240,000 generation budget + 256 x 300 recognition of every lane + 268,000 per-batch control block) / 212, each charged with its two operand loads and one result store (11,034.0), times 1/(1 - 0.001) for discarded batches = 11,045.0 (Section 4). Each word operation is charged together with its two operand loads and one result store (the data-movement upper bound of a register-free RAM program under collision-frontier-v5), so the charged total before the discard factor is 2,758.491 x 4 = 11,034.0 operations per kept pair, and 11,045.0 after it.

## 3. Landing and tail (experiment v53-shift-01)
- P1: the J39 XOR difference of a shifted pair equals the target with probability 2^-256 (premise P1). Organizer-run evidence: each trial reports landing_k8, landing_k16, landing_k24, landing_k32 and best_lead_bits over its kept pairs (expected kept x 2^-k); the organizer's seeds are fixed public seeds (fixed public seeds). On 20,000 further fresh-seed trials of the shipped program the per-bit uniformity check held on every trial (bit-match counts 3..29 of 32 tally lanes); landing k8 19,109 (expected 18,945.9), k16 57 (74.0), k24 1 (0.29), k32 0 (0.001); failed trials 0.
  - Shifted pairs of this generator and shift: 36 trials (8,749 pairs) k=8 landing 27 (expected 34.2).
  - Pooled over the 21 shifted packages R41-R61 (same generator, 183,497 pairs): k=8 landing 745 (expected 716.8), k=16 landing 4 (expected 2.80); a further 4,704 trials of the same 21 experiments on a second machine (1,138,197 pairs): k=8 landing 4,518 (expected 4,446.1), k=16 landing 14 (expected 17.37); all 1,321,694 pairs together: k=8 landing 5,263 (expected 5,162.9), k=16 landing 18 (expected 20.17); landing-rate check z = +1.39 on the pooled k=8 count (one-sided p 0.08), z = -0.27 for the unshifted program on 4,331,098 pairs.
  - The unshifted generator pairs, measured on 2^36 head-induced states, k-bit match: 2^-8.00, 2^-16.00, 2^-23.98, 2^-31.83 for k = 8, 16, 24, 32; per-bit bias at most 0.00001.
- P2: a shifted pair at the target collides after step 53 with probability 1, by construction. The shifted schedule difference is the body's difference moved by thirteen words: words 40..52 have difference 0 (checked by the schedule control on the first 16 kept pairs of every batch, 13 compares each, and on every kept pair of the 200-batch full-control run); words 29..39 carry the body's differences 16..26. The target is the state difference (0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0x80000000) at the junction after step 38; step 39 adds the lit word 39 whose difference cancels it, and from step 40 on equal words are added to equal states, so the states and the feed-forward are equal.
  - 320,000 of 320,000 (shipped program, first 16 kept lanes, 20,000 trials), 5,812 of 5,812 (evidence program, every kept lane, 24 trials) and 8,749 of 8,749 (36 trials) on real program states with the target set.
  - Negative control (bit 30 of register h): 0 in both.
- P3: landing events of distinct pairs are independent (declared). Pairs of one batch share the generator words except the retried word; after the shift no compression step is shared, and each trial is one batch of 256 lanes, so the per-trial dispersion tests the within-batch dependence. Lane-keeping dispersion over 24 batches: variance 10.14 vs binomial 13.09 (ratio 0.775), the model declared in H-kept-pair-yield. Organizer-run evidence: the per-trial landing_k8 counts of the trusted report (one batch of 256 lanes per trial) form the dispersion sample; under P3 their variance equals their mean.
  - Shifted pairs over the 4,200 second-machine trials whose per-trial outputs were retained (1,016,296 pairs; the other 504 kept only totals, k=8 508 vs 476.2 expected): mean 0.9548 vs 0.9452 expected, variance 0.9446, dispersion chi-square 4,197.8 vs 4,199 +- 91.6 (no clustering); the shipped program over 20,000 trials: kept-per-batch variance 12.61 vs binomial 12.78 (ratio 0.987).
  - VOL40 batch test of the shared generator part: 64 batches x 512 pairs, dispersion chi-square 65.0 vs 64 +- 11.3.

## 4. Ledger
- Fixed batch count: B = ceil(N / (212 x (1 - 0.001))) batches, N = (ln(1/0.61) + 2^-990) x 2^256 kept pairs. Every batch is charged at the declared generation budget 240,000 plus recognition of all 256 lanes (300 each = 76,800) plus the per-batch control block CTRL = 268,000 (controls and tails on the first 16 kept lanes, per-bit tallies on the first 32), whether a lane is kept or not and whether the batch is kept or discarded: T = B x (240,000 + 256 x 300 + 268,000) x 4 / 3988 + 4 + 2^28.2, a deterministic total-work bound. Per kept pair this is (240,000 + 76,800 + 268,000) / 212 = 2,758.491 word operations, x 4 loads/stores = 11,034.0, x 1/(1 - 0.001) = 11,045.0 charged operations = 2^1.46966 units. The shipped program runs the per-lane block only on kept lanes, so its executed work is below this charge.
- Discard rate: measured 0 of 24 batches (none under the floor, none at the budget, no hard-check failure; every failed check discards the batch and is in this count); over the 20,000 fresh-seed batches of the shipped program: 0 discarded (none under the floor, none at the budget, no hard-check failure). The declared per-batch discard probability is p <= 2.5e-4 (premise H-kept-pair-yield; the shipped program's 0 discards in 20,000 batches give the one-sided bound 2.31e-4 at alpha 0.01, the organizer's alpha, below the declared value); the declared fraction 0.001 is 4 times p; with B about 2^247.3 independent batches, Pr[more than 0.001 B discarded] < exp(-B x D(0.001 || 2.5e-4)) < 2^-1000 (D = 0.00063 nats per batch), included in the 0.39.
- T = N x 2^1.46966 + 4 x (pairs found).
- log2 N = 256 + log2(ln(1/0.61)) = 254.98345; log2 T = 256.45311 (exact 256.45310513, rounded up to five decimals).
- Success: under P1-P3 the conditional probability, given that at most a 0.001 fraction of the B batches is discarded, is at least 1 - exp(-ln(1/0.61) - 2^-990) = 0.39 + 0.61 x (1 - exp(-2^-990)) > 0.39 + 2^-991; subtracting the probability of the bad event (below 2^-1000, Section 4) leaves an unconditional success probability above 0.39. The 2^-990 margin in N changes log2 T by less than 2^-290. The run stops at the first found pair; its verification (4 reduced compressions) and the preprocessing 2^28.2 are added to T; neither changes the rounded exponent.
- Hard-asserted checks (tail53_sl.py:303-312): on every organizer trial the shipped program asserts, and fails the trial otherwise: schedule control = 16 passes on the first 16 kept lanes (40-word and shifted 53-word); sliced control = 16 passes on those lanes (J26 state and J39), and the sliced control also requires the word-level junction difference of each control lane to equal its transposed sliced difference; forced-target tail = 16 and negative control = 0 on those lanes (shifted 39..52 and unshifted 26..39); kept >= 212; generation within its budget 240,000; every one of the 256 J39 junction-difference bits matches the target bit for 16 +- 6 sqrt(32)/2 of the first 32 kept lanes (per-bit band; at 32 lanes this 6-sigma band, 16 +- 17.0, is wide and admits every count, so the uniformity evidence for P1 is the pooled count over 20,000 trials, not this check); landing_k4 <= kept/16 + 6 sqrt(kept/16) over all kept lanes. A failed trial returns null messages and reports checks_failed and checks_mask (bits 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 in the order above), so the organizer's byte-identical rerun carries these checks.

## 6. Declared premises
- P1-uniform-junction, P2-tail-induced, P3-independence, H-kept-pair-yield and I1-prng-coins, with the evidence above.
- Measured scope: joint landing to 16 bits on shifted pairs (the shipped program over 20,000 trials: k24 1 vs 0.29 expected, k32 0 vs 0.001) and to 32 bits on the unshifted generator; the full-width rate is premise P1. P2 rests on the structural argument of Sections 2-3 and the implementation checks.

- Seeds and selection: the organizer trials use the organizer's fixed public seed; our 20,000-trial runs of the shipped program use the consecutive integer seeds 300,000..319,999, fixed in the run script before the first run, with no re-draw, no discarded run and every trial reported; the 200-batch figures are the complete output of this row's earlier evidence run (seeds fixed by its script) and the 36-trial figures the complete output of its first run; no seed, run, batch or row was selected or dropped after seeing results, and the pooled cross-package count includes every shifted row.
- Random coins (declared premise I1-prng-coins): every random word of a trial is drawn from Python random.Random seeded with the trial seed (tail53_sl.py:219); the ledger treats these draws as fresh independent coins. Evidence: k=8 and k=16 landing counts over 20,000 seeds and the per-trial k=8 dispersion at expectation over 4,200 trials (Section 7).
