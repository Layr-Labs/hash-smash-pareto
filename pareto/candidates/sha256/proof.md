# VOL63: a counted cost bound for a 63-step SHA-256 ordinary collision

Score (collision-frontier-v5): time_log2 = 256.40066 target-compressions. Memory is reported separately: 2^19.92 bytes by construction (complete under the model; term max(0, log2-20) = 0).

## 1. Target and prices
- Target: two distinct messages under the standard IV, the first 63 steps, standard padding and feed-forward, all 256 output bits equal. Each message is one 64-byte data block followed by the common SHA-256 padding block; the two data blocks give an equal chaining value, so the equal padding block preserves the collision and the full messages collide.
- Prices: one 63-step target compression is one unit. Every other primitive word operation (add, sub, xor, and, or, not, shift, rotate, compare, random word, table index) costs 1/C, with C = 4828. C = 4828 is the organizer reference operation count of the 63-step compression (hash-smash/scripts/reference_operation_costs.py counting method run at 63 rounds; it gives 2896 at 40 rounds).

## 2. Head (experiment v63-shift-01)
- Generator: the VOL40 v7e generator with the W13 retry count raised from 14 to 22 outputs message pairs whose expanded difference follows the msb17 body (difference silent on words 27..39).
- Shift: each pair is replaced by its schedule words -23..-8, computed backward from the expansion rule (W_-1 = W15 - sigma1(W13) - W8 - sigma0(W0), then W_-2 = W14 - sigma1(W12) - W7 - sigma0(W_-1), and so on to W_-23 = W_-7 - sigma1(W_-9) - W_-14 - sigma0(W_-22)). The resulting words 0..15 are each member's complete 64-byte data block (equal length; standard padding appends the same final block to both); both are run from the standard IV through all 63 steps using the unmodified SHA-256 schedule recurrence and constants K_i at their original indices i (no step, word, or constant is renumbered), so the claimed event is an ordinary collision of two complete messages. The expansion of the shifted pair is the original expansion moved by 23 words, so the difference is silent on words 50..62 (checked on every pair).
- The generator, the shift, the expansion and the recomputation of the shifted pair from the standard IV work bit-sliced on 256 lanes; a gate on 256 lanes counts as one word operation, the accounting of the board's R40 entry df1f1cd1. Early abort: 4 steps before the junction the batch checks a 32-bit necessary condition that needs only half of the next step (the output a - e of a step equals T2 - d, so the two members must differ there by exactly 2^31); a batch with a live lane then computes that step and checks the register-a and register-e differences (64 bits per lane, counted).
- Each kept pair costs 12,894.0 charged operations (2^1.41721 units): 3,220.283 word operations per kept pair = (304,000 generation budget + 256 x 300 recognition of every lane + 301,900 per-batch control block) / 212, each charged with its two operand loads and one result store (12,881.1), times 1/(1 - 0.001) for discarded batches = 12,894.0 (Section 4). Each word operation is charged together with its two operand loads and one result store (the data-movement upper bound of a register-free RAM program under collision-frontier-v5), so the charged total before the discard factor is 3,220.283 x 4 = 12,881.1 operations per kept pair, and 12,894.0 after it.

## 3. Landing and tail (experiment v63-shift-01)
- P1: the J49 XOR difference of a shifted pair equals the target with probability 2^-256 (premise P1). Organizer-run evidence: each trial reports landing_k8, landing_k16, landing_k24, landing_k32 and best_lead_bits over its kept pairs (expected kept x 2^-k); the organizer's seeds are fixed public seeds (fixed public seeds). On 20,000 further fresh-seed trials of the shipped program the per-bit uniformity check held on every trial (bit-match counts 2..29 of 32 tally lanes); landing k8 18,965 (expected 18,942.1), k16 79 (74.0), k24 0 (0.29), k32 0 (0.001); failed trials 0.
  - Shifted pairs of this generator and shift: 20,000 trials (4,849,178 pairs) k=8 landing 18,965 (expected 18,942.1), k=16 landing 79 (expected 73.99).
  - Pooled over the 21 shifted packages R41-R61 (same generator, 183,497 pairs): k=8 landing 745 (expected 716.8), k=16 landing 4 (expected 2.80); a further 4,704 trials of the same 21 experiments on a second machine (1,138,197 pairs): k=8 landing 4,518 (expected 4,446.1), k=16 landing 14 (expected 17.37); all 1,321,694 pairs together: k=8 landing 5,263 (expected 5,162.9), k=16 landing 18 (expected 20.17); landing-rate check z = +1.39 on the pooled k=8 count (one-sided p 0.08), z = -0.27 for the unshifted program on 4,331,098 pairs.
  - The unshifted generator pairs, measured on 2^36 head-induced states, k-bit match: 2^-8.00, 2^-16.00, 2^-23.98, 2^-31.83 for k = 8, 16, 24, 32; per-bit bias at most 0.00001.
- P2: a shifted pair at the target collides after step 63 with probability 1, by construction. The shifted schedule difference is the body's difference moved by twenty-three words: words 50..62 have difference 0 (checked by the schedule control on the first 16 kept pairs of every batch, 13 compares each; 320,000 of 320,000 over 20,000 trials); words 39..49 carry the body's differences 16..26. The target is the state difference (0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0x80000000) at the junction after step 48; step 49 adds the lit word 49 whose difference cancels it, and from step 50 on equal words are added to equal states, so the states and the feed-forward are equal.
  - 320,000 of 320,000 (first 16 kept lanes of 20,000 trials) on real program states with the target set.
  - Negative control (bit 30 of register h): 0 of 320,000.
- P3: landing events of distinct pairs are independent (declared). Pairs of one batch share the generator words except the retried word; after the shift no compression step is shared, and each trial is one batch of 256 lanes, so the per-trial dispersion tests the within-batch dependence. Lane-keeping dispersion over 20,000 batches: variance 12.94 vs binomial 12.82 (ratio 1.009), the model declared in H-kept-pair-yield. Organizer-run evidence: the per-trial landing_k8 counts of the trusted report (one batch of 256 lanes per trial) form the dispersion sample; under P3 their variance equals their mean.
  - Shifted pairs over the 4,200 second-machine trials whose per-trial outputs were retained (1,016,296 pairs; the other 504 kept only totals, k=8 508 vs 476.2 expected): mean 0.9548 vs 0.9452 expected, variance 0.9446, dispersion chi-square 4,197.8 vs 4,199 +- 91.6 (no clustering); this program's 20,000 trials alone: chi-square 19,460.2 vs 19,999 +- 200.0; the shipped program over 20,000 trials: kept-per-batch variance 12.94 vs binomial 12.82 (ratio 1.009).
  - VOL40 batch test of the shared generator part: 64 batches x 512 pairs, dispersion chi-square 65.0 vs 64 +- 11.3.

## 4. Ledger
- Fixed batch count: B = ceil(N / (212 x (1 - 0.001))) batches, N = ln(1/0.61) x (1 + 2^-26) x 2^256 kept pairs (the factor 1 + 2^-26 covers repeated draws, Distinct pairs below, and the discard tail). Every batch is charged at the declared generation budget 304,000 plus recognition of all 256 lanes (300 each = 76,800) plus the per-batch control block CTRL = 301,900 (controls and tails on the first 16 kept lanes, per-bit tallies on the first 32), whether a lane is kept or not and whether the batch is kept or discarded: T = B x (304,000 + 256 x 300 + 301,900) x 4 / 4828 + 4 + 2^29.2, a deterministic total-work bound. Per kept pair this is (304,000 + 76,800 + 301,900) / 212 = 3,220.283 word operations, x 4 loads/stores = 12,881.1, x 1/(1 - 0.001) = 12,894.0 charged operations = 2^1.41721 units. The shipped program runs the per-lane block only on kept lanes, so its executed work is below this charge.
- Distinct pairs: the generator's pair space per lane is at least 2^274: six free message words (0, 1, 2, 3, 9 and 14; 192 bits), the seven list indices (L4 1,024, L5 128, L6 44, L7 4, L8 1,024, L10 1,024, L12 1,024; 2^54.5 combinations) and the lane's W13 word (at least 2^28 of the 2^32 words pass the W13 test, from the measured keep rate 0.946 over 32 candidates). A lane draws about 2^247.3 pairs over the run, so the expected number of repeated draws in a lane is below (2^247.3)^2 / 2^275 = 2^219.6, a fraction below 2^-27 of its draws; a repeated pair has the same landing event as its first copy and is not a new opportunity. The success bound counts distinct pairs only: N carries the factor (1 + 2^-26) > 1 / (1 - 2^-27), which changes log2 T by less than 2^-26 and leaves the rounded value unchanged.
- Discard rate: measured 0 of the 20,000 fresh-seed batches of the shipped program (none under the floor, none at the budget, no hard-check failure; every failed check discards the batch and is in this count). The declared per-batch discard probability is p <= 2e-4 (above the one-sided Clopper-Pearson 95% upper bound 1.50e-04 of 0 in 20,000); the declared fraction 0.001 is 5 times p; with B about 2^247.3 independent batches, Pr[more than 0.001 B discarded] < exp(-B x D(0.001 || 2e-4)) < 2^-1000 (D = 0.00081 nats per batch), included in the 0.39.
- T = N x 2^1.41721 + 4 x (pairs found).
- log2 N = 256 + log2(ln(1/0.61)) = 254.98345; log2 T = 256.40066 (exact 256.40065330, rounded up to five decimals).
- Success: under P1-P3 the conditional probability, given that at most a 0.001 fraction of the B batches is discarded, is at least 1 - exp(-ln(1/0.61) x (1 + 2^-26) x (1 - 2^-27)) > 0.39 + 2^-29 (distinct pairs only: at most a 2^-27 fraction of the draws repeat); subtracting the probability of the bad event (below 2^-1000, Section 4) leaves an unconditional success probability above 0.39. The factor (1 + 2^-26) in N changes log2 T by less than 2^-26. The run stops at the first found pair; its verification (4 reduced compressions) and the preprocessing 2^29.2 are added to T; neither changes the rounded exponent.
- Hard-asserted checks (tail63_sl.py:305-314): on every organizer trial the shipped program asserts, and fails the trial otherwise: schedule control = 16 passes on the first 16 kept lanes (40-word and shifted 63-word); sliced control = 16 passes on those lanes (J26 state and J49), and the sliced control also requires the word-level junction difference of each control lane to equal its transposed sliced difference; forced-target tail = 16 and negative control = 0 on those lanes (shifted 49..62 and unshifted 26..39); kept >= 212; generation within its budget 304,000; every one of the 256 J49 junction-difference bits matches the target bit for 16 +- 6 sqrt(32)/2 of the first 32 kept lanes (per-bit band; at 32 lanes this 6-sigma band, 16 +- 17.0, is wide and admits every count, so the uniformity evidence for P1 is the pooled count over 20,000 trials, not this check); landing_k4 <= kept/16 + 6 sqrt(kept/16) over all kept lanes. A failed trial returns null messages and reports checks_failed and checks_mask (bits 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 in the order above), so the organizer's byte-identical rerun carries these checks.

## 6. Declared premises
- P1-uniform-junction, P2-tail-induced, P3-independence and H-kept-pair-yield, with the evidence above.
- Not measured: bits beyond 16 jointly on shifted pairs (the shipped program over 20,000 trials: k24 0 vs 0.29 expected, k32 0 vs 0.001) and beyond 32 on the unshifted generator (P1, the full-width rate is the declared premise); a naturally landed pair (expected only after ~2^256 pairs; P2 rests on the structural argument, not on natural samples).

- Seeds and selection: the organizer trials use the organizer's fixed public seed; our 20,000-trial runs of the shipped program use the consecutive integer seeds 500,000..519,999, fixed in the run script before the first run, with no re-draw, no discarded run and every trial reported; no seed, run, batch or row was selected or dropped after seeing results, and the pooled cross-package count includes every shifted row.
