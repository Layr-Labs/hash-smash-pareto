# VOL52: a counted cost bound for a 52-step SHA-256 ordinary collision

Score (collision-frontier-v5): time_log2 = 256.48499 target-compressions. Memory is reported separately: 2^19.81 bytes by construction (complete under the model; term max(0, log2-20) = 0).

## 1. Target and prices
- Target: two distinct messages under the standard IV, the first 52 steps, standard padding and feed-forward, all 256 output bits equal. Each message is one 64-byte data block followed by the common SHA-256 padding block; the two data blocks give an equal chaining value, so the equal padding block preserves the collision and the full messages collide.
- Prices: one 52-step target compression is one unit. Every other primitive word operation (add, sub, xor, and, or, not, shift, rotate, compare, random word, table index) costs 1/C, with C = 3904. C = 3904 is the organizer reference operation count of the 52-step compression (hash-smash/scripts/reference_operation_costs.py counting method run at 52 rounds; it gives 2896 at 40 rounds).

## 2. Head (experiment v52-shift-01)
- Generator: the VOL40 v7e generator with the W13 retry count raised from 14 to 22 outputs message pairs whose expanded difference follows the msb17 body (difference silent on words 27..39).
- Shift: each pair is replaced by its schedule words -12..3, computed backward from the expansion rule (W_-1 = W15 - sigma1(W13) - W8 - sigma0(W0), then W_-2 = W14 - sigma1(W12) - W7 - sigma0(W_-1), and so on to W_-12 = W4 - sigma1(W2) - W_-3 - sigma0(W_-11)). The resulting words 0..15 are each member's complete 64-byte data block (equal length; standard padding appends the same final block to both); both are run from the standard IV through all 52 steps using the unmodified SHA-256 schedule recurrence and constants K_i at their original indices i (no step, word, or constant is renumbered), so the claimed event is an ordinary collision of two complete messages. The expansion of the shifted pair is the original expansion moved by 12 words, so the difference is silent on words 39..51 (checked on every pair).
- The generator, the shift, the expansion and the recomputation of the shifted pair from the standard IV work bit-sliced on 256 lanes; a gate on 256 lanes counts as one word operation, the accounting of the board's R40 entry df1f1cd1. Early abort: 4 steps before the junction the batch checks a 32-bit necessary condition that needs only half of the next step (the output a - e of a step equals T2 - d, so the two members must differ there by exactly 2^31); a batch with a live lane then computes that step and checks the register-a and register-e differences (64 bits per lane, counted).
- Each kept pair costs 10,883.9 charged operations (2^1.47917 units): 2,718.259 word operations per kept pair = (234,864 generation budget + 256 x 300 recognition of every lane + 264,600 per-batch control block + 7 outer-loop operations) / 212, each charged with its two operand loads and one result store (10,873.0), times 1/(1 - 0.001) for discarded batches = 10,883.9 (Section 4). Each word operation is charged together with its two operand loads and one result store (the data-movement upper bound of a register-free RAM program under collision-frontier-v5), so the charged total before the discard factor is 2,718.259 x 4 = 10,873.0 operations per kept pair, and 10,883.9 after it.

## 3. Landing and tail (experiment v52-shift-01)
- P1: the J38 XOR difference of a shifted pair equals the target with probability 2^-256(premise P1, stated for retained pairs: conditional on the pair's batch passing the floor, the budget, the hard checks and the k4 band; the k4 band is the only rule that reads landing outcomes, Section 4). Organizer-run evidence: each trial reports landing_k8, landing_k16, landing_k24, landing_k32 and best_lead_bits over its kept pairs (expected kept x 2^-k); the organizer's seeds are fixed public seeds (fixed public seeds). On 20,000 further fresh-seed trials of the shipped program the per-bit uniformity check held on every trial (bit-match counts 2..29 of 32 tally lanes); landing k8 18,849 (expected 18,940.3), k16 69 (74.0), k24 0 (0.29), k32 0 (0.001); failed trials 0.
  - Shifted pairs of this generator and shift: 36 trials (8,749 pairs) k=8 landing 33 (expected 34.2); 24 trials (5,812 pairs) k=8 landing 26 (expected 22.7), k=16 landing 0 (expected 0.09).
  - Pooled over the 21 shifted packages R41-R61 (same generator, 183,497 pairs): k=8 landing 745 (expected 716.8), k=16 landing 4 (expected 2.80); a further 4,704 trials of the same 21 experiments on a second machine (1,138,197 pairs): k=8 landing 4,518 (expected 4,446.1), k=16 landing 14 (expected 17.37); all 1,321,694 pairs together: k=8 landing 5,263 (expected 5,162.9), k=16 landing 18 (expected 20.17); landing-rate check z = +1.39 on the pooled k=8 count (one-sided p 0.08), z = -0.27 for the unshifted program on 4,331,098 pairs.
  - The unshifted generator pairs, measured on 2^36 head-induced states, k-bit match: 2^-8.00, 2^-16.00, 2^-23.98, 2^-31.83 for k = 8, 16, 24, 32; per-bit bias at most 0.00001.
- P2: a shifted pair at the target collides after step 52 with probability 1, by construction. The shifted schedule difference is the body's difference moved by twelve words: words 39..51 have difference 0 (checked by the schedule control on the first 16 kept pairs of every batch, 13 compares each, and on every kept pair of the 200-batch full-control run); words 28..38 carry the body's differences 16..26. The target is the state difference (0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0x80000000) at the junction after step 37; step 38 adds the lit word 38 whose difference cancels it, and from step 39 on equal words are added to equal states, so the states and the feed-forward are equal.
  - 5,812 of 5,812 (24 trials) and 8,749 of 8,749 (36 trials) on real program states with the target set.
  - Negative control (bit 30 of register h): 0 in both.
- P3: landing events of distinct pairs are independent (declared). Pairs of one batch share the generator words except the retried word; after the shift no compression step is shared, and each trial is one batch of 256 lanes, so the per-trial dispersion tests the within-batch dependence. Lane-keeping dispersion over 24 batches: variance 10.14 vs binomial 13.09 (ratio 0.775), the model declared in H-kept-pair-yield. Organizer-run evidence: the per-trial landing_k8 counts of the trusted report (one batch of 256 lanes per trial) form the dispersion sample; under P3 their variance equals their mean.
  - Shifted pairs over the 4,200 second-machine trials whose per-trial outputs were retained (1,016,296 pairs; the other 504 kept only totals, k=8 508 vs 476.2 expected): mean 0.9548 vs 0.9452 expected, variance 0.9446, dispersion chi-square 4,197.8 vs 4,199 +- 91.6 (no clustering); this program's 24 trials alone: chi-square 15.0 vs 23 +- 6.8; the shipped program over 20,000 trials: kept-per-batch variance 12.69 vs binomial 12.84 (ratio 0.988).
  - VOL40 batch test of the shared generator part: 64 batches x 512 pairs, dispersion chi-square 65.0 vs 64 +- 11.3.

## 4. Ledger
- Fixed batch count: B = ceil(N / (212 x (1 - 0.001))) batches, N = ln(1/0.61) x (1 + 2^-6) x 2^256 kept pairs (the factor 1 + 2^-6 covers repeated draws, Distinct pairs below, and the discard tail). Every batch is charged at the declared generation budget 234,864 plus recognition of all 256 lanes (300 each = 76,800) plus the per-batch control block CTRL = 264,600 (controls and tails on the first 16 kept lanes, per-bit tallies on the first 32) plus the 7 outer-loop operations (Section 2), whether a lane is kept or not and whether the batch is kept or discarded: T = B x (234,864 + 256 x 300 + 264,600 + 7) x 4 / 3904 + 4 + 2^29.5, a deterministic total-work bound. Per kept pair this is (234,864 + 76,800 + 264,600 + 7) / 212 = 2,718.259 word operations, x 4 loads/stores = 10,873.0, x 1/(1 - 0.001) = 10,883.9 charged operations = 2^1.47917 units. The shipped program runs the per-lane block only on kept lanes, so its executed work is below this charge.
- Distinct pairs: every pair is drawn as follows (tail52_sl.py): the six free message words 0, 1, 2, 3, 9 and 14 are uniform 32-bit draws (192 bits); the seven list words are uniform draws of an index into L4 (1,024), L5 (128), L6 (44), L7 (4), L8 (1,024), L10 (1,024) and L12 (1,024), 2^54.5 equiprobable combinations; each lane's W13 is chosen among at most 32 candidate values that are all determined by the lane's own uniform 32-bit draw (the TRY = 22 retry rounds translate that candidate and the lane keeps the first one that passes the W13 test), so a given W13 value is chosen by a lane with probability at most 32 x 2^-32 = 2^-27, whatever the pass set of its context. Two lanes of the same batch share the free and list words and differ only in W13, so they coincide with probability at most 2^-27; two pairs of different batches coincide with probability at most 2^-192 x 2^-54.5 x 2^-27 = 2^-273.5. Over the run of about 2^247.3 batches of 256 lanes (N = 2^254.98 draws) the expected number of repeated draws is at most 2^247.3 x 32,640 x 2^-27 + N^2 / 2 x 2^-273.5 < 2^235.3 + 2^235.5 < 2^236.5, a fraction 2^-18.5 of N; by Markov's inequality the repeated fraction exceeds 2^-7 with probability at most 2^-11.5. A repeated pair has the same landing event as its first copy and is not a new opportunity, so the success bound counts distinct pairs only: N carries the factor (1 + 2^-6) > 1 / (1 - 2^-7), and the 2^-11.5 bad event is subtracted below. Every list index (nine draws per batch: seven base words, the W17 target and the W25 target) is drawn by a bounded rejection loop in the shipped program (_bdraw): a k-bit draw, k the bit length of the list size, is accepted if it is below the list size and redrawn otherwise, at most 32 attempts, after which the last draw reduced modulo the list size is used. The ledger charges the worst case, 9 x 32 x 3 operations (draw, compare, branch) = 864 operations per batch, included in the generation budget above; the fallback is taken with probability at most 2^-32 per draw and yields a valid list element, so the time bound is deterministic.
- Landing-dependent discards: of the discard rules, only the k4 band (landing_k4 <= kept/16 + 6 sqrt(kept/16), checked over all kept lanes) depends on landing outcomes; the floor, the budget and the hard checks do not. A lane whose pair lands exactly also matches 4 prefix bits, so its batch is discarded only if the other kept lanes alone bring the k4 count to the band's edge. Under P1 and P3 the other lanes' 4-bit matches are independent Bernoulli(1/16) indicators, so with kept >= 212 the probability of that, given the exact landing, is at most Pr[Bin(255, 1/16) >= 34] < 2^-13 (Chernoff). An exactly landed pair is therefore counted as an opportunity with probability at least 1 - 2^-13, which the factor (1 + 2^-6) in N covers together with the repeated draws (2^-7 + 2^-13 < 2^-6 / (1 + 2^-6)); the 2^-11.5 Markov event is subtracted below.
- Discard rate: measured 0 of 24 batches (none under the floor, none at the budget, no hard-check failure; every failed check discards the batch and is in this count); over the 20,000 fresh-seed batches of the shipped program: 0 discarded (none under the floor, none at the budget, no hard-check failure). The declared per-batch discard probability is p <= 2.5e-4 (premise H-kept-pair-yield; the shipped program's 0 discards in 20,000 batches give the one-sided bound 2.31e-4 at alpha 0.01, the organizer's alpha, below the declared value; the interval supports the declared value, it is not the premise); the declared fraction 0.001 is 4 times p; with B about 2^247.3 independent batches, Pr[more than 0.001 B discarded] < exp(-B x D(0.001 || 2.5e-4)) < 2^-1000 (D = 0.00063 nats per batch), included in the 0.39.
- T = N x 2^1.47701 + 4 x (pairs found).
- log2 N = 256 + log2(ln(1/0.61)) + log2(1 + 2^-6) = 255.00585; log2 T = 256.48499 (exact 256.48498941, rounded up to five decimals).
- Success: under P1-P3 the conditional probability, given that at most a 0.001 fraction of the B batches is discarded, is at least 1 - exp(-ln(1/0.61) x (1 + 2^-6) x (1 - 2^-7 - 2^-13)) > 0.39 + 0.0022 (distinct pairs only, when at most a 2^-7 fraction of the draws repeat, which holds except with probability 2^-11.5, and with the k4-discard loss of at most 2^-13 per landed pair; Distinct pairs and Landing-dependent discards above); subtracting the probability of the bad event (below 2^-1000, Section 4) and the 2^-13.5 repeat bad event leaves an unconditional success probability above 0.39 (0.39 + 0.0022 - 2^-11.5 - 2^-13 - 2^-1000 > 0.3915). The factor (1 + 2^-6) in N adds 0.02240 to log2 T. The run stops at the first found pair; its verification (4 reduced compressions) and the preprocessing 2^29.5 are added to T; neither changes the rounded exponent.
- Hard-asserted checks (tail52_sl.py:303-312): on every organizer trial the shipped program asserts, and fails the trial otherwise: schedule control = 16 passes on the first 16 kept lanes (40-word and shifted 52-word); sliced control = 16 passes on those lanes (J26 state and J38); the sliced control also requires the word-level junction difference of each control lane to equal its transposed sliced difference; forced-target tail = 16 and negative control = 0 on those lanes (shifted 38..51 and unshifted 26..39); kept >= 212; generation within its budget 234,864; every one of the 256 J38 junction-difference bits matches the target bit for 16 +- 6 sqrt(32)/2 of the first 32 kept lanes (per-bit band; at 32 lanes this 6-sigma band, 16 +- 17.0, is wide and admits every count, so the uniformity evidence for P1 is the pooled count over 20,000 trials, not this check); landing_k4 <= kept/16 + 6 sqrt(kept/16) over all kept lanes. A failed trial returns null messages and reports checks_failed and checks_mask (bits 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 in the order above), so the organizer's byte-identical rerun carries these checks.
